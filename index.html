MACROBASE REPOSITORY

Select the exact macros you want to include on your printer.

How to use MacroBase:

  • Browse: Look through the categories below to find macros that fit your needs.
  • Select: Click individual macros to add them to your bundle. You can also click a category title to instantly select or deselect all macros within that group.
  • Download: Once you are happy with your selection, scroll to the bottom and click the download button.
  • Install: Extract the downloaded folder directly into your Klipper configuration directory and add [include MacroBase.cfg] to your printer.cfg file.
